# FAQ

**Which radios?** One package for all: 800 × 480 (X20 family, X18R, X18RS), 640 × 360 (X14), 480 × 320 (X18, Twin X Lite), 480 × 272 (X10, X12S). Only the X18RS has been tested on a real radio, the others in the simulator.

**Do I need Rotorflight?** No. Only the Governor field, ARMED/SAFE and profile/rate need Rotorflight values; otherwise these spots stay empty or follow the motor switch.

**How is Battery % calculated without a % sensor?** From the cell voltage at rest when the battery is plugged in (by battery type), then counted down with the used mAh. Cells and Capacity must be set.

**How do I reset the used capacity?** Tap **RESET** in the field, use Battery › Reset used capacity, or Reset flight in Ethos.

**Can I use it without touch?** Yes, via the menu group «Flights without touch» and the rotary encoder.

**Why do swiping and the rotary encoder show the flight list?** Intended: on this screen they page through the flight list. Swipe left once more or press PAGE for the next Ethos screen.

**Does the widget replace my own checks?** No. The alarms are an aid; check battery and temperature yourself.
